Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective 20 September 2026. Covers the automated publishing tool used by the National Premier League (Cricket), Joburg Hockey League, and Tshwane Prime League (Netball).

NPL Social Automation is an internal tool that publishes match photos, graphics, results, and highlight videos to these three youth sports leagues' own Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts, and — for National Premier League content only — YouTube channel. This page explains what data it touches, and just as importantly, what it doesn't.

What this tool does

  • Reads match photos, graphics, and video clips that team photographers and league administrators upload to a shared Google Drive folder.
  • Reads fixture and result data from CricClubs, the cricket scoring and fixtures platform the league already uses.
  • Generates a caption for each piece of content.
  • Sends every draft to a private Telegram group, where a league administrator reviews and approves it before anything goes out.
  • Publishes the approved post to the relevant league's Facebook Page, Instagram account, and, for National Premier League content, YouTube channel — using access tokens the Page and channel owners granted directly.

What this tool does not do

  • No public-facing login, sign-up, or account system — nobody outside a small group of league administrators interacts with it.
  • No collection, storage, or processing of personal data from anyone who views, follows, likes, or comments on the Pages or accounts it posts to.
  • No cookies, analytics, or tracking of the public, in any form.
  • No selling, sharing, or transferring of data to third parties.

Data it does handle

The only data this tool processes is operational sports content — match photos and videos, team names, age groups, scores, and fixture details — supplied directly by each league's own photographers and administrators for the purpose of publishing it. Player and team names may appear in published photos and captions the same way they would in any ordinary sports report or match-day graphic.

Questions about this policy

Email hzlwebstudios@gmail.com and a league administrator will respond.

Instructions

User Data Deletion

There is no account system behind NPL Social Automation, so there is no personal data on file to delete on that basis. This section covers the one situation that can still come up: a specific published post.

If you believe you, or someone you represent, appear in a photo, video, or caption this tool has published, and you'd like it removed, send a request and it will be handled directly — this doesn't go through Facebook, Instagram, or YouTube's own data-deletion tools, since no account data is held there in the first place.

To request removal

Email hzlwebstudios@gmail.com with:

  • A link to the specific post, and
  • A short description of what you'd like removed.

Requests are handled within 5 business days of receipt.
